## Approach and Implementation
I read the following papers about fault injection methodology in other experiments and Bayesian fault injection to help figure out our experiment design: Chen et al., Alemzadeh et al., Jha et al., and Coelho et al.

I met with Dr. Cao on Tuesday to discuss future directions and how to implement Bayesian fault injection. We met again on Friday with Dr. Srikant to discuss the more theoretical side of the project, as up until now it has been relatively high level. We realized that there may be hidden faults that are affecting results that we have not considered, and are looking at using factor graphs, k-SAT, causal inference/do-calculus, and uncertainty sampling to move forward with the project. 

I added a problem statement to our paper draft, and created figures for experiment design, fault injection workflow, and an experiment tree. In addition, I started modeling secure key rate and QBER mathematically as a function of our fault parameters, and realized that QBER is an intermediate variable, along with number of surviving bits and sifting time, as well as an output variable. This helped me understand how the fault-error-failure pipeline applied to my project.
